# Data

## Networks

The customer regions (name, state, latitude, longitude, population) and fulfillment centers (code, state,
latitude, longitude) are the files of the public repository of Ma (2023),
https://github.com/willma353/multi_item_e_commerce_fulfillment, unchanged:

- `small_network/cities_warmup.csv`, `small_network/fulfillment_centers_warmup.csv`: 10 regions and 5 centers (Sections 6.1-6.3).
- `large_network/cities.csv`, `large_network/fulfillment_centers.csv`: 99 regions and 10 centers (Section 6.4).

## Small network (Sections 6.1-6.3)

Generated by `code/small/make_instances.py`, which can regenerate and check every file.

- `instances/<case>/<T>/model.npz`: the instance (n, M) = `I<n>_M<M>` at horizon T: `type_ptr`, `action_ptr`,
  `resources`, `reward` (divided by the largest reward of the instance), `p`, `ell` (the constants L_j of (3.6));
  see `code/small/model.py`. `inventory.npz`: `capacity`, the initial inventory B_T of each item-center pair
  (resource i * 5 + c for item i and center c).
- `demand/<case>/path_000.npz` ... `path_029.npz`: `arrivals`, 100,000 request types; a run with horizon T uses the
  first T.
- `fluid_lp/<case>_<T>/`: optimal solution `x` and dual prices `price` of the fluid LP with demand T p and inventory
  B_T, with its optimality certificate (`code/small/compute_fluid_lp.py`).

The instance of Section 6.4 is generated by `code/huge/build_instance.py` and is not stored.
